The Game

Basilika is a tactical strategy Trading Card Game built for the love of the concept and vision. You build a deck around a single Archetype and use unique tile cards to construct the board fresh every match.

The Game consists of familiar elements found in many other card / board games, but woven together with intricate detail.

A game where the board is built fresh every time you play. Where your card placements and piece movements create razor sharp positions, and the layered strategy packed within the game makes every match feel fresh and intense.
